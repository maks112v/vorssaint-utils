# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mac users who want a focused set of everyday utilities without installing, configuring, and paying for a separate app for each task. They use Vorssaint from the menu bar while working in other apps and expect each tool to stay quick, local, and out of the way.

## Product Purpose

Vorssaint combines optional Mac utilities behind one menu bar icon. Users install only the features they need, keep their data on the Mac, and avoid accounts, telemetry, and subscriptions. Success means a utility completes its job with little setup, no idle cost after removal, and no surprise network or permission use.

## Brand Personality

Direct, capable, and considerate. Copy explains what a feature does in plain words. The interface should feel native to macOS and confident enough to stay quiet while the user's work remains primary.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic web-dashboard styling, decorative cards and pills, ornamental motion, hidden resource costs, vague permission requests, and custom controls that fight familiar macOS behavior. Do not make every feature look equally important or permanently visible.

## Design Principles

- Keep the user's current task in front. Floating tools should occupy only the space their current state needs.
- Install and reveal capability on demand. Inactive features should disappear and stop consuming resources.
- Use familiar macOS controls and interaction patterns unless a custom control makes the task measurably faster.
- State permissions, network use, energy use, and destructive effects before they happen.
- Prefer one clear action over persistent explanation or decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native accessibility labels, keyboard commands, system appearance, and semantic colors. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep controls understandable without relying on color alone. Localized layouts must tolerate longer labels across every supported language.
